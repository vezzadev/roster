# Bundle C — Buyer / WTP Signals (raw, from Researcher 2026-05-22)

## C1. Public IDR pricing
### MileApp (https://mile.app/en/pricing — scraped 22 May 2026)
- Free: IDR 0 — 1,000 tasks/mo.
- Pro: **IDR 2,500,000/mo (~US$155 at IDR ~16,100/USD)** — 2,500 tasks/mo, IDR 1,000/extra task.
- Enterprise: custom.
- Illustrative: 10K tasks = IDR 10M/mo; 15K = IDR 14M/mo.
- Homepage logos: **Unilever, Brinks, Mitsubishi, P&G, Sampoerna, POS Indonesia** — enterprise, not SMB. Important: their bootstrapped status sits on enterprise customers, not SMB.

### Jubelio (https://jubelio.com/pricing — scraped 22 May 2026)
- Flat **IDR 150/order processed** (~US$0.009).
- No seat/feature/store/user limits.
- 10K orders/mo → ~US$93; 100K → ~US$930.
- Claim: "Trusted by 35,000+ businesses across Indonesia."
- Note: e-comm OMS/WMS, not 3PL TMS — but the *only* clear public IDR price benchmark for logistics-adjacent SaaS to SMBs. Confirms Indonesian SMBs accept granular usage-based billing.

### McEasy (https://www.mceasy.com/faq-mceasy-platform/ — 25 Jan 2024)
- Two tiers: McEasy Plus (mobility), McEasy Pro (full TMS).
- **No public list prices** — custom/negotiated. Enterprise-leaning sales motion (200+ clients).

### HashMicro
- No public pricing. "Request a demo." Enterprise direct.

## C2. Indonesian SMB digital adoption / WTP
**Primary source**: Mastercard + Mercy Corps + 60 Decibels *Small Business Barometer Report* (fieldwork Nov 2023-Jan 2024; published 27 Jun 2024). N=835 MSEs.
- 70% use digital tools (mostly e-commerce platforms 46%, wallets/banking 34%).
- 81% recognize digital tools as important for growth.
- 64% don't know which digital tools fit their business.
- **31% cite cost as prohibitive barrier**; 38% cite digital literacy gap.
- Finance management tools = top demand (34% first pick).
- Kominfo digital literacy index 2022: 3.54/5 = "moderate."
- Source: https://www.mastercard.com/news/ap/en/newsroom/press-releases/en/2024/empowering-indonesia-s-small-businesses-insights-and-strategies-for-overcoming-digital-and-financial-barriers/
- **Caveat**: study covers MSEs in F&B/fashion/crafts/tourism, NOT logistics. Logistics SMBs likely better resourced — directionally higher WTP but the literacy/selection-paralysis pattern likely persists.

## C3. Pricing anchors for our client
- MileApp Pro = IDR 2.5M/mo (~US$155) is a **plausible SMB TMS price ceiling** in ID.
- Jubelio model shows usage-based pricing is acceptable to Indonesian SMBs.
- A US-list price of US$200-500/mo translates to IDR 3.2-8M/mo — **above MileApp Pro**.
- Add PPN-PMSE 12% (passes to buyer) + potential PPh-26 10% WHT shrinkage on collected revenue.
- **31% cost-barrier stat**: localized IDR pricing and likely a step down from US list will be needed to penetrate SMB.

## Implications for recommendation
- Realistic SMB ARPU ceiling in ID is materially below US ARPU.
- WTP gap argues for: lighter packaging (Starter at IDR 1.5-2M ~ US$100), usage component, no $-invoicing.
- Buyer education / discovery is a real cost — "64% don't know which digital tools fit" — so partner/channel education matters as much as product.
- Enterprise upmarket (like MileApp's actual customer base) may be where the money is — but our client targets SMB. Tension to address in the brief.
