## A1. GDP & Economic Outlook
- **World Bank IEP "Digital Foundations for Growth"** (16 Dec 2025): GDP grew **5.0% in 9M2025**; projected ~5.0% through 2026–27.
  - URL: https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/12/16/indonesia-s-economy-maintains-resilience-amid-global-uncertainty

- Commercial estimates (secondary, flagged): Mordor $131B, IMARC $40.7B — different definitions, NOT primary.

- Reuters 20 Jan 2026 URL: https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/why-has-indonesian-rupiah-hit-record-low-despite-dollar-weakness-2026-01-20/

## A7. e-Faktur friction (E-Ask supplement)
- Indonesian B2B buyers cannot claim input VAT credit on PMSE-VAT paid to foreign providers (no e-Faktur issued by foreign SaaS).
- Establishing a PT PMA and registering as PKP enables proper e-Faktur and eliminates this buyer friction.
- This is a real incentive to form local entity earlier than the IDR 600M threshold alone would suggest.
