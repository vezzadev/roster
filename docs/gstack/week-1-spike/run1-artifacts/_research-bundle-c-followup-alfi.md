# C-followup — ALFI on digitisation (raw, Researcher 2026-05-22)

## Source
- OceanWeek (Indonesian maritime trade press), 3 Jul 2025 — coverage of ALFI CONVEX 2025 launch at Ministry of Economic Coordination.
- URL: https://oceanweek.co.id/alfi-luncurkan-alfi-convex-akan-gelar-pameran-di-bsd-tangerang-pada-november-2025/

## Quotes
- **M. Akbar Djohan, Ketua Umum DPP ALFI**: *"Sektor logistik merupakan jantung dari transformasi menuju Indonesia Emas 2045... sektor logistik yang efisien dan terintegrasi adalah fondasi utama..."*  ("Logistics is the heart of the Indonesia Gold 2045 transformation; an efficient, integrated logistics sector is the foundational requirement.")
- **Coordinating Minister for the Economy Airlangga Hartarto**: *"Kita perlu mendorong deregulasi di sektor logistik... peringkat logistik kita saat ini masih berada di posisi menengah—**61 dari 139 negara**."*  ("We must push deregulation in logistics; our LPI ranking is mid-tier — **61st of 139 countries**.")

## Hard data
- ALFI membership: **4,300 companies across 33 provinces**.
- Logistics sector GDP contribution: **4.6% (2020) → 6.6% (2024)** per Menko Airlangga.
- ALFI CONVEX 2025 conference theme included: *"efisiensi logistik dan digitalisasi rantai pasok"* (logistics efficiency + supply-chain digitisation).
- World Bank LPI: Indonesia ranked **61/139** (per Menko's statement; will cross-check with WB direct in Bundle A).

## Implications
- Strong **demand-side credibility anchor**: industry association (4.3K members) + government minister both name digitisation as a priority, on the same stage, in 2025.
- 4,300 ALFI members is the most concrete addressable-market anchor for the brief's sizing language.
- The 4.6→6.6% GDP figure is a useful "the sector is growing while still underperforming on LPI" framing.
