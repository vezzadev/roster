## Caveats to flag in brief
- GetLatka ARR figures for McEasy/MileApp are self-reported and not audited; should be cited as "self-reported via GetLatka" or treated as directional only.
- TiA Facebook post on Waresix cash is paywalled; treat as secondary.

## Implications for recommendation
- Category is in **post-hype consolidation**: capital has stopped flowing to most pure-plays since 2022.
- **Kargo is the only credible 2024 winner** but it's an asset-heavy / marketplace play, not pure SaaS — different model than our client.
- **McEasy is the closest analogue** to our client's product (SMB-focused SaaS TMS). If their self-reported $11.5M ARR is even directionally right, that's a useful upper bound on what a focused SMB-TMS can do in ID. It also implies the market exists but isn't enormous.
- **Foreign vertical SaaS has not won SMB**; only enterprise (Locus/Shipsy) — channel & localization gap.
- **Bootstrapped MileApp's existence** suggests low-touch, low-ARPU products can sustain — argues for capital-light entry mode.
