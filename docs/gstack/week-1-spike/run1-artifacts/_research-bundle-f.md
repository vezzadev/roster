# Bundle F — Foreign SaaS Entry Precedents (raw, from Researcher 2026-05-22)

## F1. Salesforce — Full PT PMA entity + Country Leader
- **Aug 2023**: PT Salesforce.com Indonesia legal entity launched.
- **Jul 2024**: First Country Leader (Iman Muhammad) named; office One Pacific Place, Jakarta. ~12–18 months from entity to country leader.
- Pattern: entity-first (compliance: PSE/PMSE) → leadership second. On DJP PMSE collector list.
- Source: Antara News, 27 Jul 2024 — https://www.antaranews.com/berita/4220403/salesforce-umumkan-pemimpin-kawasan-pertamanya-di-indonesia

## F2. Zoho — Country Lead + authorized reseller channel
- Country Lead: Handito Aji Saroso. Active at MarketingFest 2024, DTI-CX 2025, Zoholics Indonesia 2026.
- Authorized partners: Indo Krisna Teknologi, Lab Anak Bangsa.
- No confirmed PT PMA entity — likely operates via Singapore entity + PSE registration.
- Sources: Exabytes MarketingFest 2024 — https://www.exabytes.co.id/events/acara/marketingfest-2024 ; LinkedIn Indo Krisna — https://id.linkedin.com/in/rifqiae ; Instagram Zoholics ID 2026 — https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWnXZoGyl1b/

## F3. Freshworks — Singapore-managed, no Jakarta office
- Coverage via Singapore regional hub (Keith Liew LinkedIn: "GTM across Singapore and Indonesia").
- Event-based presence (TechConnect Jakarta).
- Source: https://sg.linkedin.com/in/keith-liew-73b237a0

## F4. Locus.sh — Indonesia content/demand-gen; ACQUIRED Oct 2025
- Targets large enterprises (retail, FMCG, e-comm, 3PLs) — explicitly not SMB.
- **Oct 2025**: Acquired by Ingka Group (IKEA). Operates independently but strategically tied to IKEA.
- Source: Locus press release — https://locus.sh/press-releases/ingka-group-acquires-locus/ ; PR Newswire — https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/locus-ushers-in-a-new-era-of-agentic-transportation-management-302612419.html ; Locus blog (Sep 2025) — https://locus.sh/blogs/top-logistics-software-companies-indonesia/

- G2 verbatim: "350+ team based out of India, Dubai, Riyadh, Africa, Netherlands, and Indonesia serving 230+ customers."
- Reviews skew enterprise (80 large / 49 mid-market / 28 small biz) — Indonesia presence serves enterprise, not SMB.
- Source: https://www.g2.com/products/shipsy-shipsy/reviews

## F6. Anchanto — Singapore-origin, regional model, enterprise-only
- Customers: DHL Supply Chain, NinjaVan, Toll Logistics, L'Oréal, HP, Panasonic, Decathlon.
- Sep 2022 product expansion + global push.
- Source: https://anchanto.com/en-gb/launches-four-saas-products-new-brand-identity/

## F7. PSE enforcement is real and current (CRITICAL FINDING)
- **31 May 2025**: Komdigi (formerly Kominfo) warned 36 PSE non-compliant companies including **Apple, DHL, eBay, Lazada, Epic Games, Garmin, Xbox, Emirates, Nike, HP, Philips, Unilever, KLM, Lenovo, McDonald's, Zurich, Traveloka, JNE**.
- Prior precedent: Jul-Aug 2022, Yahoo / PayPal / Valve (Steam) / Nintendo / Ubisoft temporarily blocked for non-registration.
- DHL on the warn list = direct logistics-sector precedent.
- Sources: Tempo English, 31 May 2025 — https://en.tempo.co/read/2012852/indonesia-targets-36-unregistered-private-pse-for-possible-ban ; CCIA filing to USTR, 29 Oct 2025 — https://downloads.regulations.gov/USTR-2025-0016-0053/attachment_1.pdf

## F8. Tookan (Jungleworks) — only SMB-priced foreign logistics SaaS
- Self-serve / PLG model.
- USD pricing: $99/mo for 1,000 tasks.
- Listed in Locus's Indonesia top-10 blog as SMB/mid-market option.
- Pattern: no local entity / no local team — pure PLG.
- Source: https://locus.sh/blogs/top-logistics-software-companies-indonesia/

## F9. ITA US-government guidance (primary)
- US ITA Country Commercial Guide for Indonesia, Market Entry Strategy, **updated 17 Nov 2025**.
- Verbatim: "U.S. companies are strongly encouraged to collaborate with reputable local partners who understand the nuances of regulatory compliance, local consumer behavior, and distribution networks... patience, persistence, and a sustained local presence are highly valued."
- Verbatim: "Indonesian buyers remain price-sensitive but increasingly demand value-added services, such as product customization, competitive payment terms, reliable technical support, and localized after-sales service."
- Source: https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/indonesia-market-entry-strategy

## Implications for recommendation
- **Entry mode taxonomy from precedents**:
  1. *Full PT PMA + Country Leader* (Salesforce): big-ticket, ~18 months sequencing, used by enterprise SaaS at $100M+ revenue scale. **Overkill for our client right now.**
  2. *Country Lead + Authorized Resellers, no PT* (Zoho): partner-led, low fixed cost, suited to SMB/mid-market. **Closest fit for our client's size.**
  3. *Singapore regional hub, no entity* (Freshworks): cheapest, but limited local presence. **Possible for a "test the water" Year-1.**
  4. *Local team, no confirmed PT* (Shipsy): India peer; enterprise-only.
  5. *Pure PLG / self-serve* (Tookan): the only SMB-priced foreign logistics SaaS — but no evidence of meaningful Indonesia revenue.
- **No precedent of a US vertical logistics SaaS winning SMB in Indonesia via direct sales.** The two viable paths are partner-led (Zoho pattern) or PLG self-serve (Tookan pattern) — and likely *both* in combination.
- **PSE enforcement is active and current** — Komdigi warned DHL etc. as recently as May 2025. PSE registration must be done at launch, not "later". This is a hard prerequisite.
- **ITA guidance is unambiguous**: partner-led, sustained local presence. Confirms a partner-first / hybrid approach over direct.
