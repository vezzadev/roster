# D-followup — SaaS-as-Royalty Tax Classification (raw, Researcher 2026-05-22)

## Source (best public treatment found)
- Deloitte SE Asia / Indonesia, *"The challenges of software distribution payments: a tax perspective"*, **published 1 Dec 2025**. Authors: Shivaji Das, Yohanes Janitra Jaya, Anggaris Anggia Cininta P.
- URL: https://www.deloitte.com/southeast-asia/en/services/tax/perspectives/id-software-taxation-2025.html

## Core finding
- **The ambiguity is real and actively disputed.** Deloitte: *"Some distributors have faced recurring adjustments, sometimes a large share of annual revenue, due to differing interpretations of what constitutes a royalty versus a business profit."*
- **Law No. 7/2021** (Harmonization of Tax Regulations) defines royalty broadly — "compensation for use of or right to use various forms of IP or technical rights." This is **broader than the OECD model**, creating mischaracterization risk.

## Characterization test (Deloitte)
- **Redistribution → business income** (no Indonesian WHT if no PE) — rights to distribute software as finished product.
- **Exploitation → royalty** (PPh-26 WHT: 20% standard / 10% w/ US treaty + CoD) — rights to reproduce, adapt, exploit.
- **SaaS subscription**: under OECD ¶14.4 and Deloitte's analysis, should be business income — but Indonesian domestic law's broader definition + no DJP ruling means it's a live audit risk.

## Practical risk for our client
- **If treated as royalty**: Indonesian B2B customer must withhold 10% (US treaty w/ CoD) / 20% (no CoD). Forces a **gross-up or margin hit** on every Indonesian invoice.
- **If treated as business income**: no customer WHT — but DJP auditor may disagree later.
- **APA (Advance Pricing Agreement)** under PMK-172/2023 available as risk mitigation, but only economic once revenue is meaningful.

## Implications for brief
- Cite Deloitte as primary public analysis; flag risk as "live, unresolved."
- Year-1 pricing model should **assume 10% royalty WHT applies** (conservative case). At list price US$200/mo, realized revenue per customer = US$180/mo before VAT/CIT.
- A pricing-table line item: "Add ~11% effective IDR PPN-PMSE (passed to buyer) + 10% PPh-26 royalty WHT (absorbed or grossed up by us)."
