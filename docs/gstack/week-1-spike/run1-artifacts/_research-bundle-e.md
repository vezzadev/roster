## E3. Mandatory employment costs
- **THR**: 1 month gross/yr (religious holiday allowance, mandatory)
- **BPJS Ketenagakerjaan** (workforce ins): ~5.74% gross (employer)
- **BPJS Kesehatan** (health): 4% gross (employer, capped ~IDR 12M base)
- **PPh 21**: employer withholds; up to 30% marginal
- **Pesangon (severance)**: up to 2× monthly × years of service (Cipta Kerja 2020)
- **Total employer multiplier: ~1.18–1.25× gross**

## E5. Payment rails (CRITICAL)
- **Stripe NOT available** for Indonesia-domiciled businesses.
- **Xendit**: 2.9% + IDR 2K (cards); IDR 4K flat (virtual account). Subscription billing API.
- **Midtrans (GoTo)**: similar.
- **BI-FAST**: real-time bank rail, IDR 2,500/txn, max IDR 250M/txn. 2024 vol IDR 1,760T (+55.4% YoY).
- Cross-border before PT: USD wire works but pushes PMSE/PPh-26 friction onto Indonesian buyer.
- Source: https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/performance-indonesias-digital-payment-transactions-2024-qzvnc ; BI press release https://www.bi.go.id/en/publikasi/ruang-media/news-release/Pages/sp_2627524.aspx
