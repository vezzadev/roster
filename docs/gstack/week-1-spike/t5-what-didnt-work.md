# What Didn't Work

Running journal of dead ends, abandoned approaches, and surprising failures during the Week 1 spike. Append-only diary.

Parent: [../design.md](../design.md)

## Why this file matters

Codex tension T2 (motivated reasoning at the failure moment) is mitigated by writing failures down as they happen — not summarized in retrospect. A failed approach with a timestamp is data; a forgotten one is repeated.

## Format

Each entry is an H2 with date + short title. Body covers:
- **What I tried:**
- **Why I thought it would work:**
- **What happened:**
- **What I changed (if anything):**
- **Cost in time + tokens:**
