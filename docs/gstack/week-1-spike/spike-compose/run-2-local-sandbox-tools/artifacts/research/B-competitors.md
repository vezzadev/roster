# Thread B: Local & Regional Competitors Targeting Indonesia Logistics SMBs
_Researcher first-pass — 2026-05-23_

---

## Summary

Of the seven named Indonesian logistics-tech firms, **two confirmed active (Waresix, Kargo Technologies)** as of mid-2025; **two appear defunct (Logisly, Ritase)** based on dead domains; **one active with e-commerce focus (Shipper.id)**; **one active supply chain SaaS (Advotics)**. Aptrindo domain has been hijacked by a gambling site — unusable. Andalin could not be scraped (all engines failed). Regional players (Anchanto, Parcel Perform, Locus.sh, Detrack) are checked below with partial results. The primary direct competitor to the client's vertical-SaaS TMS/WMS product is **Kargo Nexus** (TMS SaaS, 40,000-truck network, enterprise-tier pricing unknown) and **Waresix WOS** (AI-backed operating system, end-to-end logistics platform with SaaS SKU).

## Indonesian Players

### 1. Waresix
- **Status:** ACTIVE (latest blog post September 4, 2025; company website fully operational as of 2026-05-23)
- **Founded:** 2017 (started as WMS/warehousing, expanded to trucking 2019, end-to-end 2021)
- **Self-description:** "Platform Logistik Terbesar di Indonesia" (Largest Logistics Platform in Indonesia)
- **Product offerings:** Land transport, domestic shipping (inter-island), heavy equipment delivery, **SaaS Solution (WOS)**, warehousing & logistics terminals
- **WOS (Waresix Operating System):** Machine learning + AI; features include omnichannel order creation, smart matching, real-time analytics, e-Invoice & e-POD, digital assistant (LINDA)
- **Certifications:** ISO 27001:2022 (Information Security Management, obtained 2024)
- **Awards:** CNBC "Most Inspiring Startup" (2022), KPMG "Top 50 Emerging Giants in SEA" (2022), Granite Asia "NextGen Tech 30" (2024)
- **Key customers mentioned:** GoKomodo (Supply Chain Director), Sociolla (Logistics Manager), Heinz ABC (POD Manager)
- **Scale indicators:** JS-animated counters (couldn't resolve exact numbers), 13+ MT Cargo/Year was the only static value visible
- **Milestones:** 2023 expanded to domestic container depot in Surabaya; 2024 ISO certification
- **CEO:** Andree Susanto (Founder); CFO: Edwin Wibowo (Co-Founder)
- **Sources:** https://waresix.com/ and https://waresix.com/tentang-kami/ (accessed 2026-05-23)

### 2. Kargo Technologies (Kargo Nexus)
- **Status:** ACTIVE (website fully operational, EV truck partnership news appears to be from 2024–2025)
- **Founded:** 2018 (per website: "Berdiri sejak 2018")
- **Scale:** 
  > "The company, which operates a network of **40,000 trucks** and serves **more than 200 enterprise clients**"
  _(embedded press excerpt from TechInAsia on Kargo's website)_
- Also states: **1,500+ companies served**, **8,000+ trusted drivers**, **100+ transporter networks**
- **Kargo Nexus (TMS SaaS):** 
  - Full TMS with: Real-time GPS track & trace, geofencing, fleet tracking, Instant POD (e-POD), bulk upload, fleet management, auto-freight audit, ESG/carbon footprint reporting, API integration with SAP/Oracle/ERP
  - Available for both shippers (Nexus for Shipper) and transporters (Nexus for Transporter)
  - Described as "SaaS (Software as a Service)... targeting 3PLs, manufacturers, distributors"
- **EV expansion:** Planning to deploy 500 EVs in 2025/2026 and 2,500 by 2026; piloting in Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand to follow
- **Key customers:** Danone Indonesia, Sea's SPX, Astro, AirAsia's Teleport
- **CEO:** Tiger Fang (co-founder, Silicon Valley background per website)
- **Pricing:** Not publicly listed
- **Source:** https://www.kargo.tech/ and https://www.kargo.tech/tms-nexus (accessed 2026-05-23)

### 5. Shipper.id
- **Status:** ACTIVE (website fully operational; blog/case studies reference 2024 events)
- **Focus:** Primarily B2C/e-commerce fulfillment, not B2B freight-forwarding TMS for 3PLs
- **Key stats:**
  - **120+ warehouses** across Indonesia
  - **2,000+ merchants supported**
  - **10+ e-commerce channels integrated**
  - **99.8% SLA**
- **Services:** International freight forwarding (China, Thailand, Vietnam, Japan, Australia), fulfillment, contract logistics, logistics aggregator, e-commerce enabler (Aloshop), supply chain financing (Pintek)
- **Awards:** FastCompany "Most Innovative Company in Asia-Pacific, #1 APAC" (2022); CNBC "Most Inspiring Aggregator" (2021)
- **Positioning vs. client:** Shipper targets e-commerce brands/retailers, NOT the 3PL-operator or freight-forwarder segment the client's product serves. Low overlap.
- **Source:** https://shipper.id/ (accessed 2026-05-23)

### 6. Advotics
- **Status:** ACTIVE (website operational; YouTube channel active)
- **Product:** Supply chain SaaS suite — Workforce Management System, Distribution Management System, Warehouse Management System, Route Management System, Delivery Planning System, Vehicle Management System, Product Digitalization (QR/RFID)
- **Target markets:** Manufacturers, Distributors, 3PLs, Consumer goods companies, SMEs (via distri.id)
- **Key customers:** PT Mitra Rubber Industries, Nutrifood (from website testimonials)
- **HQ:** North Jakarta (Gold Coast Office, Penjaringan)
- **Pricing:** Not publicly listed
- **Positioning vs. client:** Direct overlap — Advotics sells TMS/WMS/DMS SaaS in Indonesia to the same segment (3PLs, distributors). Has an SME product variant (distri.id)
- **Source:** https://www.advotics.com/ (accessed 2026-05-23)
